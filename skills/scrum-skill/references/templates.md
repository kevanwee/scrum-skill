# Adaptable artifact templates

Read/copy only records needed by the current operation. Persist them in the project's
chosen record directory. Fields labelled proposed/pending are not approvals.

## Product and Sprint backlog

Product Goal: future outcome, user and boundary. State and decision provenance.
Ordered items: ID, user value, conversation/source, acceptance criteria, estimate by
Developers, dependencies/uncertainty and evidence plan. Preserve existing item IDs.
Sprint backlog: one Goal (why), selected items (what), delivery tasks (how), dates,
capacity assumptions, current plan and next integration action.

## Story

As a [abstract user role], I want [action], so that [outcome].
Conversation: actual clarification, assumptions and responsible decision-maker.
Confirmation: observable happy/negative/boundary scenarios, evidence and scope.
Estimate/uncertainty: Developers' forecast, not an AI-invented promise.

## Shared Definition of Done

Product/organization quality requirements, acceptance evidence, integrated behavior,
required reviews/checks, documentation and recovery appropriate to scope. Record the
version, accountable reviewers and evidence source; source merge/acceptance/release
are separate. Tailor rather than transplant a legal or frontend project's safeguards.

## Sprint Planning

Actual participants/inputs and whether this is preparation or an event; proposed or
agreed state; Product Goal; one Sprint Goal; why/what/how; dates/timebox; capacity and
assumptions; selected slices/dependencies/estimates; DoD version; deferred scope and
open decisions. Developers own their plan; PO owns backlog ordering and value.

## Daily adaptation

Goal progress and evidence, plan changes/next action, impediments/owners, decisions
needed. Distinguish a Developers' actual Daily Scrum from async agent preparation;
never fabricate attendance or three-question status theater.

## Sprint Review

Actual participants/feedback state; Goal; integrated revision/DoD; Done outcomes and
demonstration evidence; changed circumstances; backlog adaptation. Unfinished items
are status/return-to-backlog, not an Increment demo. Include applicable release state.

## Retrospective

Actual inputs; what helped/hindered; disproved assumptions; quality/collaboration/DoD;
a few owned improvements with due date and observable measure; inspect previous actions.
Keep this separate from product feedback in Review.

## Evidence, impediments and decisions

Evidence: item/criterion, required check, exact revision/environment/result, limitation.
Impediment: observed obstacle, impact on Goal, owner, next action, needed-by/status.
Decision: question/options, accountable decision-maker, actual decision/source/date,
consequence. Distinguish defects, evidence gaps and pending judgments.

## Handoff and fresh-session prompt

Handoff: date/timezone, active Sprint/items, changed paths/revision, tests/evidence,
decisions, open issues/owners, needs and next action. LATEST: current state in a few
lines and exact newest record links. Master prompt: applicable instructions, record
map, Goal/DoD/current plan, last evidence, authorization/decisions, open issues and the
next useful step. Do not copy private records into public commits/issues.
